Privacy Policy for Catan Classic

Developer: Quantum Core Studio • Effective Date: October 31, 2026

At Quantum Core Studio, we are committed to providing a transparent, fair, and fun gaming experience. This Privacy Policy details how Catan Classic handles information when you install and enjoy our digital board game.

1. Account-Free Local Gameplay

Catan Classic does not require or support user account registration. You can play single-player AI matches and pass-and-play sessions immediately without creating an account, providing an email address, or logging in.

All gameplay progress, pioneer stats, custom bio text, audio settings, and earned in-game credits are saved locally on your device via standard Android application storage.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party Ad Services (Google AdMob)

To keep Catan Classic free to download and support future game expansions (such as upcoming custom map packs), the game displays advertisements served by the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK.

When ads are displayed or loaded, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personal device information, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID), device hardware model, and operating system version.
  • Location Data: General / coarse location derived from IP address (e.g., country or region) to serve geographically relevant ads.
  • App Performance & Diagnostics: Ad load events, interaction logs, and crash metrics used to monitor stability and prevent fraudulent impressions.

For more information on how Google manages and protects advertising data, please review:
• Google Privacy Policy
• How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Their Services

3. Data Encryption in Transit

All data transmitted between the application and third-party advertising or network services is strictly encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS) protocols.

4. In-Game Store, Currency & Ad-Free Memberships

Players can earn in-game credits by voluntarily choosing to watch rewarded video ads or by claiming the one-time welcome bonus. These credits can be redeemed in the Frontier Emporium to activate temporary or permanent ad-free memberships:

  • Bronze Pass: 24-hour ad-free pass (100 credits).
  • Silver Pass: 7-day ad-free pass (550 credits).
  • Gold Pass: 30-day ad-free pass (1,800 credits).
  • Diamond Sovereign: Permanent lifetime ad immunity (5,000 credits).

When an ad-free pass is active, in-game banner, native, and interstitial ads are suppressed.

5. How to Control or Opt Out of Interest-Based Advertising

You have full control over your advertising identifiers on Android. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time via your device settings:

  • Open your phone's Settings app.
  • Navigate to Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  • Select Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Catan Classic does not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If we become aware that personal information from a minor has been inadvertently collected without verified parental consent, we will promptly purge it.

7. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in legal requirements or new gameplay features. We encourage players to review this page occasionally. Continued use of the game following updates constitutes acceptance of the modified policy.
